# Survey Guru — Into the Future Master Plan v1.0

**Product Owner:** TES — Task Expert Systems  
**Operational / Field Partner:** Taskraft (Pty) Ltd  
**Status:** Strategic Master Plan / Living Document  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Survey Guru will be developed as more than a digital survey and data-capture application. The long-term objective is a continuously learning market-intelligence and field-execution platform capable of understanding physical markets, identifying gaps in market knowledge, directing field resources, identifying commercial opportunities, and ultimately connecting market opportunity with route-to-market execution.

**Discover → Survey → Validate → Map → Analyse → Find Gaps → Direct Field Teams → Re-survey → Learn**

> Every field activity should make the TES intelligence layer more knowledgeable than it was before.

The initial deployment will solve immediate Taskraft operational requirements, particularly WTS and FMCG market-survey activities, while preserving the architecture required for the future TES platform.

## 2. TES and Taskraft Ecosystem

Survey Guru and Fleetwize are products of **TES — Task Expert Systems**. Taskraft and TES operate as a coordinated ecosystem while maintaining appropriate corporate, commercial, security, data and intellectual-property boundaries.

**Taskraft operational problem → TES scalable capability → Taskraft deployment → Real-world experience → TES product improvement.**

Survey Guru focuses on market/outlet/geographic/field intelligence. Fleetwize focuses on distribution, routes, vehicles and route optimisation. Taskraft provides operational execution, consulting, field teams and the real-world proving environment.

## 3. Permanent Outlet Universe

> **The outlet is a permanent entity. A survey is an observation of that outlet at a particular point in time.**

Outlets survive individual projects. Visits and observations build history against the same physical market entity. Client-confidential information remains segregated and only contributes to broader TES intelligence where contractual, privacy and data-use rights permit it.

## 4. Know What We Do Not Know

Survey Guru should measure confidence in its understanding of geographic markets: high confidence, medium confidence, low confidence and unknown. It should progressively answer:

> **What don't we know, and where should we go to learn it?**

## 5. Intelligent Fieldwork

Fieldwork should progress from static assignments toward dynamically generated work based on assigned outlets, verification visits, geographic coverage zones, probable outlets and priority opportunities. AI assistance may later support outlet classification, signage/brand/SKU recognition, duplicate detection, image validation and GPS anomaly detection.

## 6. Survey Guru Coverage Engine

The first major intelligence engine should answer:

> **Where should we survey next?**

Potential inputs include existing outlets, permitted historical WTS data, geographic and road data, population density, commercial nodes, permitted client locations, survey density, discovery rates and prediction accuracy. The platform should progressively calculate coverage confidence and expected value of additional field investigation.

## 7. Learning Loop

**Prediction → Field Deployment → Observation → Comparison → Model Adjustment → Improved Prediction**

Where legally and contractually permissible, field operations should improve TES intelligence over time.

## 8. Survey Guru ↔ Fleetwize Bridge

Survey Guru asks: **What exists in the market and where is the opportunity?**

Fleetwize asks: **How should the distribution network service that market?**

Together they should evolve into a broader TES route-to-market decision system.

## 9. Commercial Evolution

### Stage 1 — Survey Execution
Client requirement → Taskraft surveys → analyses → reports.

### Stage 2 — Technology-Enabled Execution
Taskraft deploys Survey Guru for faster, better-controlled and more efficient field execution. TES supplies the technology.

### Stage 3 — Intelligence-Assisted Consulting
Survey Guru identifies insights beyond the immediate survey request and supports Taskraft consulting recommendations.

### Stage 4 — Proactive Opportunity Identification
Survey Guru detects opportunities, TES quantifies them, Taskraft validates commercial relevance and proposes interventions. **Intelligence starts generating projects.**

### Stage 5 — Intelligence as a Product
Potential models include project intelligence, subscription intelligence, territory monitoring, expansion intelligence, sales opportunity intelligence, verification services and authorised API/data services.

### Stage 6 — Decision Intelligence
Survey Guru and Fleetwize combine market and distribution intelligence to progress from **Here are the outlets** → **Here are the opportunities** → **Here is the recommended route-to-market decision.**

## 10. Data Flywheel

Taskraft field operations → Survey Guru → TES intelligence → Taskraft consulting/execution → Survey Guru measurement → Fleetwize → TES decision intelligence → improved future decisions.

The objective is not simply to accumulate data. It is to improve **decision quality**.

## 11. Data and Client Trust

Survey Guru distinguishes:

- **Client-Owned / Client-Private Data**
- **TES Product Data**
- **TES Reference Intelligence** where rights permit
- **Public / Licensed Data**

Client data must never silently become shared market intelligence merely because it passes through Survey Guru.

## 12. Technology and Security Principles

Development must maintain separation between Development, Staging/Test and Production. Customer data must not be casually used for experimental development. Secrets remain outside repositories. Least privilege applies.

> **No autonomous agent receives simultaneous authority over code, production credentials and deployment.**

> **Security boundaries must be enforced independently of the UI. The UI is UX, never an access-control boundary.**

All protected data/actions require strict API/backend/database-side authorisation.

## 13. Human-in-the-Loop Principle

**AI recommends → Human verifies → System records outcome → Model learns.**

Automation may increase as confidence and controls mature.

## 14. North Star

Survey Guru should progressively answer four questions:

1. **What is there?**
2. **What don't we know?**
3. **Where should we go next?**
4. **What should the client do?**
