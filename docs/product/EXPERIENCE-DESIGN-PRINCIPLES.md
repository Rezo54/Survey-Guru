# Survey Guru Experience Design Principles

**Status:** Locked product direction  
**Owner:** TES — Task Expert Systems  
**Product:** Survey Guru

## Product idea

Survey Guru should make complex field intelligence feel inevitable.

The product experience follows a simple progression:

**Capture truth → Reveal opportunity → Tell the story.**

Over time that becomes:

**Idea / hypothesis → Story → Execution → Evidence → Progress → Outcome → New story.**

A project should be able to show not only what the team believes today, but how the evidence changed the story and whether the intervention later worked.

## Experience qualities

- Capturing should feel effortless.
- Evidence should feel trustworthy.
- Insight should feel revelatory.
- Opportunity should feel tangible.
- Presentation should feel unforgettable.

The field worker should be asked only for information that requires human judgement. Location, timestamps, movement, known outlet identity, prior observations, validation and duplicate signals should be derived or prefilled where reliable.

## Intelligence progression

Every meaningful analytical experience should move through:

**Finding → Evidence → Meaning → Opportunity → Action.**

Never show data merely because Survey Guru has it. Show it because it changes understanding or action.

## Opportunity Mapping

Opportunity Mapping is a hero capability. Geography is not decoration and must not be reduced to a generic dashboard widget.

The product should progressively reveal:

**Unknown → Searched → Discovered → Understood → Opportunity → Action.**

Maps should hide irrelevant complexity at the moment of decision and bring forward the evidence that explains why an area, outlet or cluster matters.

## Story + Explore

Story and Explore are two modes over the same authorised intelligence.

**Story** creates understanding. It is a curated, evidence-led progression that reveals the market, search, findings, surprise, opportunity, recommendation and later outcome with deliberate pacing.

**Explore** creates conviction. It allows an authorised client or operator to interrogate the same evidence, geography, outlets, photographs, coverage, QA and opportunity logic.

Story must never become a presentation detached from authoritative data. Explore must never bypass project scope or data rights.

## Visual restraint

Survey Guru should not imitate another company's visual language. The desired essence is disciplined product craft:

- generous space;
- exceptional typography;
- restrained use of controls;
- beautiful, purposeful cartography;
- deliberate photography;
- meaningful motion rather than decoration;
- one dominant thought at a time;
- progressive disclosure instead of information dumping.

The interface should have the confidence to hide everything that does not deserve attention at that moment.

## Security and authority

Simplicity in the UI must never weaken authority boundaries. The UI is not a security boundary. Story, Explore, maps, dashboards and cached PWA state are all untrusted clients of the Survey Guru API.

Every protected read, write, export or analytical query must be authorised server-side according to identity, workspace, permission, project/assignment scope, data domain and data rights.

## MVP discipline

These principles guide the architecture now, but the first local/Firebase checkpoint remains deliberately narrow. Build the field/PWA/API/Android foundation and representative map-first surfaces first. Do not delay the checkpoint for a full Story builder, advanced animation system or speculative reporting features.

The desired client reaction remains simple:

**“I did not know that about my market.”**
